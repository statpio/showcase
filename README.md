## Add your website

Hosting a site on Statpio? Open an [issue](https://github.com/statpio/showcase/issues/new) with the website's name, one line on what it is, its address and what it was built with, and we'll add it. Business sites, portfolios, tools, landing pages and AI-built sites are all welcome; client sites under an agency's brand too, with the client's permission.

## About Statpio

Every website on Statpio gets hosting on Cloudflare's global network with automatic SSL, form capture with a no-code form builder, cookieless analytics, password protection and rollbacks. Paid plans add custom domains, GitHub push-to-deploy and a hosted blog; Agency Pro adds a client portal under the agency's own brand and domain. Sites can be published as a ZIP, from GitHub, from Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor through the Statpio connector, or with `npx statpio deploy`.
